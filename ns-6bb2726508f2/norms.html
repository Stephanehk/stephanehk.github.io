--- sitemap: false layout: null --- {% raw %} Own Values vs Social Norms

The LMs critique actions that break social norms; ACT, SFBT and MI critique actions that go against the user's own values

758 vignettes from run argon_n1000 that are clearly inconsistent or consistent on both situation dimensions: the user's own view of whether the situation fits their personal values, and the vignette's LM view of whether it fits prevailing social norms (neutral levels left out). For each rater, a linear probability model of critique on both indicators with robust (HC1) standard errors; effects are in percentage points. LM responses labelled by the Argon judge (revised action prompt); experts are Argon agent-experts labelling what the response should do. Social norms are conveyed less reliably by the vignette text than own values (readback 76% vs 99.9% exact), which would weaken, not create, the LMs' social-norms effect.
