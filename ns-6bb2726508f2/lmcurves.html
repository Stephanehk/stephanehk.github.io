How many more vignettes?

Learning curves for predicting each LM's judged response from the situation vector, 997 vignettes of the argon_n1000 run. 10 November 2026.

For each LM and each response dimension, a model is trained on k vignettes to predict the LM's judged label from the situation vector and scored on 197 held-out vignettes. Error is the held-out log loss divided by the log loss of always predicting the base rate: 1.0 means the situation vector tells us nothing, lower is better. Solid lines are measured; dotted lines are a power-law forecast out to 3,000 vignettes; the dashed vertical line marks the vignettes we have.

Medians over 26 LM-by-dimension curves. The noise on one measured point at 800 is about 0.008 for the regression and 0.02 for the trees.

  • With each situation dimension acting on its own, the curves have flattened: another 1,000 vignettes is forecast to lower error by 0.014.
  • Interactions are still being learned, mostly for the action stance (Opus 5.5 is forecast at 0.70 with 1,000 vignettes, 0.65 with 2,000 and 0.63 with 3,000).
  • Emotional expression, empowerment, questioning and the framing stance (Argon, Opus 5.5) stay at 0.9 or above; the situation vector does not explain them at any size.
  • The forecasts are extrapolations from seven points between 100 and 800, and the tree results rest on 6 random splits (20 for the regression).
