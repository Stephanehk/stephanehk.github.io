Do we have enough vignettes?

Two diagnostics for a stopping rule on vignette generation. The first asks whether the sampler is still finding new regions of the situation space. The second asks whether more labelled vignettes still improve how well the situation vector predicts what the agent-experts prescribe. 580 design vignettes, Opus v2 expert labels, 2026-10-29.

1. The sampler is nowhere near filling the space

Each point is one vignette's weighted distance to the closest vector generated before it, anchors included, in generation order. The line is a rolling median over 25 vignettes. Random sampling with the same gap mix is shown for scale. The distance is the sampler's own: framework coordinates and the six user-minus-LM gaps at weight 2, LM and user-only appraisals at weight 1, so a full swing on one weight-1 appraisal is distance 2.

2. Learning curves: does the vector predict the experts better with more vignettes?

One multinomial logistic regression per school and response dimension, from the 25 one-hot-coded situation coordinates (78 columns, no interactions, L2 with C fixed at 1.0) to the expert's label. In each of 50 repeats the vignettes are shuffled, 128 are held out, and nested training prefixes of 25 to 450 vignettes are fit from scratch. Blue is mean held-out accuracy with a one-standard-deviation band across repeats; the dashed line is the majority class of the training subset. "Last 100" is the paired gain from 350 to 450 training vignettes, mean and standard error over repeats.

Reading the curves

  • Still rising (gain over the last 100 training vignettes at least two standard errors above zero): ACT emotional support, MI emotional support, Person-centered emotional support, SFBT emotional support, ACT action guidance, SFBT action guidance, ACT framing guidance. About one accuracy point per 50 vignettes, not yet slowing.
  • Flat, above baseline: CBT emotional support, CBT action guidance. The vector carries signal and the current sample has extracted it.
  • At or below the majority class at 450: MI action guidance, Person-centered action guidance, CBT framing guidance, MI framing guidance, Person-centered framing guidance, SFBT framing guidance. Whether these schools take a position is not predictable from the vector at any n, so more vignettes of this design will not help; either the decision depends on something the 25 coordinates do not encode, or the labels are noisy on this question. Label repeats would separate the two.
