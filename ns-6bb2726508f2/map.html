Situation map

Each point is one of the 100 vignettes, placed by t-SNE on its situation features. Every panel uses the same positions. Each panel is colored by one rater's label on one response dimension. Hover over a point to highlight the same vignette in every panel; click to keep it highlighted.

Features: LM verdict and confidence, the 15 user appraisals, the 6 LM appraisals as LM minus user, a missing-verdict flag, and one-hot phase and goal (31 features, unscaled, so distance is in scale points). Only neighborhoods are meaningful in t-SNE: the distance between two clusters and their sizes are not. Layout: perplexity 30, seed 0.
